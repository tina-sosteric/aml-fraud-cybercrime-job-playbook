# Breaking Into AML, CTF, Fraud & Cybercrime

**The Complete Job Search Playbook** (free PDF, version 1.1)

A free guide for people who want to get into anti-money laundering (AML), CTF, fraud, trust & safety and cybercrime work. Made by Ari.0.0.1.

**Download:** `AML_CTF_Fraud_Cybercrime_Job_Search_Playbook_v1.2.pdf` (in this repo, and under Releases)

---

## Why I made this

I have skills and experience in fraud investigations (crypto/fintech), and a lot of people have reached out asking how to get into this field. Many more never dare to send a message. So I wrote everything down in one place, to make it a bit easier.

I also recently became part of the cybersecurity community, where so much is open source and shared for free, and I'm very grateful for it. I love this mindset. Anyway, this is me giving back from the fraud side.

## What's inside

- ATS and CV tips, plus how to use AI to tailor your CV honestly
- LinkedIn profile, posting and recruiter outreach
- Visibility and referrals (a weekly routine and a 6-week timeline)
- Target employers by sector, not only crypto
- Job boards, hidden channels and how to check company culture
- Recruitment and fake interview scams (please read this part)
- Certifications, free courses, regulation basics and a glossary
- CV bullet examples, interview questions and sample answers
- A prompt for practice interviews with AI
- A one-page cheat sheet at the end

## IMPORTANT: the scenarios are made up

**All interview scenarios and sample answers in this playbook are imaginary.** They sound realistic on purpose, but they are invented. They did not happen. They are not based on real cases, real customers, real employers or my own work.

In your own interviews, please be careful about what you share, respect any NDA you signed, and ask the interviewer for an imaginary scenario ("how would you solve this?") instead of describing a real case. Interviewers should respect that too.

## Not for fraudsters

If you were hoping this teaches you how to commit fraud or get around detection: it won't help you, sorry. It is a job search guide for the people on the defending side. The scenarios are "what would I do if..." answers, not tricks, and there are no real detection rules, thresholds or internal procedures from any company in here. Save your time.

## Not a magic wand

This playbook does not guarantee a job. Take what helps, then work out what fits you.

- It's made from my own notes and handbooks, training materials and courses I've taken, and lessons from job hunting and interviews.
- The notes were merged and tidied up with AI, so it's a partly AI-generated guide.
- I tried to check every link when I wrote it, but websites move, close and change owners. Double-check every URL and research every company yourself before you apply.
- Company, course and certification names belong to their owners. Mentioning them is not an endorsement.
- It's career guidance, not legal, regulatory or compliance advice.
- I'm not responsible for anything in it or for how you use it.
